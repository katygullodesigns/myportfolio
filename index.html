ABOUT ME

I’m an entry-level Graphic Designer with a passion for turning ideas into meaningful visuals. I love exploring color, composition, and the small design details that make a piece feel intentional. My work blends creativity with problem-solving, and I enjoy creating designs that feel both expressive and thoughtfully structured.

I’m proficient in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, Lightroom, and Acrobat, and I enjoy experimenting with different tools from digital illustration and vector graphics to web design in Figma. I also have hands-on experience with photography, image retouching, and traditional print methods like cyanotype, silk-screening, and heat press.

My design interests range from branding and packaging to layout, typography, and social media content. I’m always excited to learn new techniques, try new styles, and collaborate on projects that challenge me to grow.
